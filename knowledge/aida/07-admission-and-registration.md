## Who can join

The programme is designed for **complete beginners**:

- No coding knowledge is required.
- No previous programming experience is required.
- No technical background is required.
- A **laptop is mandatory**. Joining on a mobile phone alone is not sufficient.

## How to register

1. Contact **AIDA Reception** (see Contact) and choose your batch (1st–10th, 11th–20th or 21st–30th).
2. Pay the course fee (see Course Fees and Payment).
3. AIDA verifies your payment and then **confirms your registration**. A payment screenshot alone is not proof of confirmed payment.

The **Register Interest** form on this website is for receiving updates only. It does not reserve a place.
