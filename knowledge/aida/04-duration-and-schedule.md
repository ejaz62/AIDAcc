- **Duration:** 10 days. One or two days off may be arranged depending on convenience.
- **Monthly batches:** 1st–10th, 11th–20th and 21st–30th of each month.
- **Normal class timing:** 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time (PKT).
- **Recordings:** provided for revision and catching up. They do **not** replace the attendance expectation.
- **Attendance:** students are expected to attend at least **8 of the 10 days**.
