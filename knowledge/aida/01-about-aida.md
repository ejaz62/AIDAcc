**AIDA – Artificial Intelligence Digital Academy** teaches students practical artificial intelligence skills through beginner-friendly, hands-on learning.

- AIDA currently offers **one confirmed programme**: the **AIDA AI Training Programme**.
- The programme is designed for **complete beginners**. No previous coding, programming or technical experience is required.
- Classes are **live and instructor-led, online**.
- AIDA emphasises **practical learning**: demonstrating real AI projects, explaining concepts step by step and helping students build practical projects themselves.
