## During the course

- **Recordings** of classes are provided for revision and catching up (they do not replace attending).
- **Your work is yours:** students retain their project files and source code.
- **Questions:** contact AIDA Reception by phone or email (see [Contact](/contact)).

## After the course: learn from each other

- AIDA encourages students to **create their own WhatsApp groups** to exchange knowledge with one another.
- New students are encouraged to **join groups formed by previous students**, where they can consult experienced seniors, share experiences and learn from one another. They can also create their own groups.
- These groups are created and run by students. **AIDA does not manage or take part in them**, and they are not an official AIDA support service.
