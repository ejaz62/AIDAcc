## Cancellation before the first class

If you change your mind **after paying but before attending the first class**, AIDA will **refund your course fee**.

- Only the **actual bank or payment-transfer charges** incurred when sending the refund may be deducted.
- If **no bank or transfer charges** are incurred, **nothing is deducted** and you receive **100% of the course fee**.

## Refund after the first day's class

If you are **dissatisfied after completing the first day's class**, AIDA provides a **100% refund** of the course fee, **without asking questions**.

## No hidden deductions

- No percentage deductions.
- No administrative fee.
- No cancellation penalty.
- Bank charges are never estimated in advance — only charges actually incurred may be deducted.
