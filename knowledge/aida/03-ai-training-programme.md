AIDA currently offers **one confirmed programme: the AIDA AI Training Programme.** The subjects listed under Curriculum are learning areas within this programme, not separate courses.

## At a glance

- **Duration:** 10 days
- **Delivery:** live, instructor-led online classes
- **Batches:** every month: 1st–10th, 11th–20th and 21st–30th
- **Class time:** 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time
- **For:** complete beginners. No coding, programming or technical background needed

## What you take away

- Hands-on experience building practical projects with AI tools.
- How to **publish websites online**.
- You **keep your project files and source code**.
- An **AIDA certificate** when you successfully complete the course.
