Q: How many courses does AIDA offer?
A: AIDA currently offers one confirmed programme: the AIDA AI Training Programme.

Q: How much is the course fee?
A: Pakistan: PKR 15,000. Saudi Arabia: SAR 200. International: USD 55.

Q: How long is the course?
A: The programme runs for 10 days, with one or two days off depending on convenience.

Q: What are the class timings?
A: Classes normally run 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time. Batches run on the 1st–10th, 11th–20th and 21st–30th of each month.

Q: Is coding knowledge necessary?
A: No. The programme is designed for complete beginners.

Q: Do I need a laptop?
A: Yes, a laptop is mandatory. A mobile phone alone is not sufficient.

Q: Which AI tools do I need?
A: The free version of ChatGPT is sufficient. A Claude Pro subscription is required for the training workflow.

Q: Will there be recordings?
A: Yes, recordings are provided for revision and catching up, but students are expected to attend at least 8 days.

Q: Are there any discounts?
A: AIDA's discount policy has not yet been confirmed.

Q: What if I am not satisfied with the course?
A: If you are not satisfied after the first day's class, your full course fee is refunded without any questions asked.

Q: Will I get a certificate?
A: Yes. Students who successfully complete the course receive an AIDA certificate.

Q: How do I pay?
A: In Pakistan, pay via Easypaisa to 0322 7204898 (account holder Muhammad Ejaz). Your registration is confirmed after AIDA verifies the payment; a screenshot alone is not proof of payment.

Q: What will I learn?
A: Claude and AI tools, AI-powered website development, AI agents and their deployment, AI employees, WhatsApp AI assistants and practical AI automation, including how to publish websites online.
