The AIDA AI Training Programme covers these practical learning areas:

1. Claude and AI tools
2. Website development using AI
3. AI agent development
4. AI agent deployment
5. AI agents and AI employees
6. WhatsApp AI assistants and employees
7. Practical AI automation

Students also learn how to **publish websites online**, and they keep their project files and source code.

## Teaching approach

- Demonstrating practical AI projects
- Explaining concepts step by step
- Helping beginners understand how AI tools work
- Guiding students to build practical projects themselves

A detailed day-by-day timetable has not been published.
