What you can work towards after the programme:

- **Build websites using AI tools** — from idea to a working site.
- **Create AI agents and AI employees** that handle real tasks.
- **Automate workflows** to save time on repetitive work.
- **Publish and deploy websites** online yourself.
- **Use the skills in your own business, your job or freelancing.**
- **Keep everything you build** — your project files and source code are yours.

AIDA provides practical training. It does **not** promise employment, guaranteed earnings, clients or business success; results depend on your own practice and effort.
